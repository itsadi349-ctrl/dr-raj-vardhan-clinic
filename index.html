Frequently Asked Questions

Common inquiries about OPD fees, rapid lab tests, fasting rules, and diabetes care.

1. What is the OPD Token registration fee? Do I need to pay online in advance? ▼

The registration fee is ₹100 only. There is ZERO advance online payment required on this portal. You simply reserve your token online and pay the ₹100 fee at the clinic counter upon arrival.

2. How fast do I get my HbA1c and Blood Sugar lab reports? ▼

Our in-house rapid diagnostic lab delivers accurate HbA1c (3-month blood sugar average) in just 10–12 minutes, allowing same-day consultation with Dr. Raj Vardhan without any multi-day delay.

3. How many hours of fasting are required for blood sugar and lipid tests? ▼

Fasting blood sugar and lipid profiles require 10 to 12 hours of overnight fasting. Drinking plain water is allowed, but strictly avoid tea, milk, biscuits, or morning breakfast before the blood draw.

4. What are the early signs of Diabetic Neuropathy in feet? ▼

Burning sensation, needle-pricking, tingling, or numbness in the feet are early warnings of nerve impairment. Dr. Raj Vardhan conducts 10g monofilament sensory screenings to diagnose and prevent non-healing diabetic ulcers.
